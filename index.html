How we work

Three standards. Everything that carries our name has to meet all of them.

From the source

Every figure, rule and specification comes straight from the organization that publishes it. We check it against the published page, update it when the source changes, and always tell you where it came from.

Plain words

Every screen explains what you are looking at and where the numbers come from. We describe what our products do today, not what we hope they will do someday. This page follows the same rule.

Yours stays yours

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code in anything we ship. Nothing you enter leaves your device unless you choose to send it.
