How we work

Three standards, for every field we work in. Everything that carries our name has to meet all of them.

From the source

Whatever we build starts from the authority on it: the published figure, the governing rule, the specification, the measurement. We check our work against that source, keep up when it changes, and say where it came from.

Plain words

Everything we make explains itself: what you are looking at, what it does, and where it came from. We describe what exists today, not what we hope to make someday. This page follows the same rule.

Yours stays yours

Nothing we make collects anything about you. No accounts, no tracking, no advertising. What you put into our work leaves your hands only when you choose to send it.
