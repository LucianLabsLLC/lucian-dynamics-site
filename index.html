How we work

Three rules. Everything that carries the name has to keep all of them.

From the source

Every figure, rule or specification comes from the body that publishes it, is checked against that page, and is updated when the publisher's figure changes. We say which source, every time.

Plain words

A screen says what a thing is and where it came from. We describe what our work does today, not what we hope it will do next year. This page keeps that rule too.

Yours stays yours

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code in anything we ship. Nothing you enter leaves your device unless you send it yourself.
