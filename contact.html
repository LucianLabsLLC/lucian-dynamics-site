The company

paygradesupport@icloud.com

The privacy policy explains what our software stores, downloads and sends, and the support page answers the most common questions about PayGrade.

Billing is Apple's. PayGrade Premium is sold through the App Store, so Apple handles payments, renewals and refunds. To change or cancel the subscription, use Settings, then Manage Subscription in the app; to request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. The support page walks through it.

What happens to your email

We receive your address, your message and anything you attach, and we use them only to reply to you and fix what you reported. They are never shared with anyone, and if you ask, we delete the correspondence. The privacy policy says the same in full.
