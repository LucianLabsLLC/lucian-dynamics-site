PayGrade: from inside the app

Settings has three rows that already know your app version and device, so you never have to look them up:

  • Report a Bug opens an email to support with your app version, device and pay data already filled in. In versions that attach the diagnostics log, you can delete the attachment before sending.
  • Suggest a Feature opens an email to paygradefeedback@icloud.com.
  • Diagnostics shows the small on-device log of what the app did (screens, taps and stalls, never what you typed). Share opens the system share sheet with the log attached so you can send it with a report, and Clear empties it. Nothing is sent unless you send it yourself. The privacy policy describes the log in full.

PayGrade: common questions

The calendar asks for a pay date. Why?

Federal pay periods are the same government-wide, but pay dates depend on your payroll provider: NFC, DFAS, IBC and GSA don't all pay in the same week. A calendar built on a guess would be a week off for many people, so PayGrade asks you once for a recent pay date (Home, then Schedule, then the settings icon) and counts from there.

Where do the figures come from, and are they current?

Pay figures come from OPM's General Schedule, locality and special-rate tables. Tax figures come from the IRS and the states, contribution limits from the TSP, and benefit figures from the Social Security Administration. At most once a day, the app checks a page we publish for newer tables and figures, digitally signed so the app can confirm they came from us, and keeps them only if they verify. Settings, then About, shows which data the app is using.

Everything the app shows is an estimate. Confirm anything that matters with your HR office or OPM.

The estimate doesn't match my statement.

The Take-Home Estimator has a statement card: type in the lines from one Earnings and Leave Statement, or read them from its PDF, and the app shows where the estimate differs, line by line. If a line is clearly wrong, send a bug report with your series, grade, step and locality and the line that differs. You don't need to send your statement.

Managing, restoring or cancelling Premium

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store, so Apple handles the billing. To change or cancel it, use Settings, then Manage Subscription in the app, which opens Apple's subscription screen, or open the Subscriptions section of your Apple Account in the iOS Settings app. Cancelling keeps Premium until the end of the period you have paid for.

On a new device, or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases on the Premium screen. Refund requests go to Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

If you bought the lifetime unlock in an earlier version, it is still honored: Restore Purchases brings it back. If it doesn't, email us.

What happens if Premium ends?

Nothing you entered is deleted. The Premium screens lock, and any Premium schedule or leave marks are kept and shown again when you resubscribe. Events PayGrade added to Apple Calendar may be removed when the subscription ends. Your own events are never touched.

Export to Apple Calendar

With your permission, PayGrade creates a calendar named "PayGrade" and adds your work days, pay days, holidays and leave to it. It only ever adds, changes or removes events it created itself, and the export screen in the app can remove them at any time. Export is part of Premium.

Location and notifications

Both are optional, and neither is requested when the app starts. Location, enabled under Settings or with the location button in the Take-Home Estimator, finds your state, and where it matters your county or city, for the income tax estimate. Notifications, enabled under Settings, are step-increase reminders the app schedules on your device. The app works fully with both declined.

Which devices does PayGrade run on?

iPhone and iPad. The App Store page lists the iOS version it requires.
