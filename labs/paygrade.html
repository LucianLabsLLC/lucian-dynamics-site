PayGrade: the pay calculator for federal employees on the General Schedule.

Enter your series, grade, step and locality. PayGrade shows your base pay, locality adjustment, gross pay and an estimate of what you take home, from OPM's own tables that the app keeps current by itself.

What PayGrade does

PayGrade: GS Pay Calculator runs on iPhone and iPad. It is built around how federal pay, schedules and benefits actually work, and it uses the figures OPM, the IRS, the TSP and the SSA publish.

Free

Calculator

  • Base pay, locality adjustment and gross pay for every GS series, grade, step and locality pay area, Rest of U.S. included.
  • Special rates, including the ones that depend on your agency.
  • Find your locality by town name.
  • Overtime, Sunday premium, night differential and the premium pay cap.
Premium

Take-home pay

  • Federal, state, county and city income tax, with every state's brackets, deductions and exemptions.
  • FERS, TSP, FEHB, FEGLI and your W-4.
  • Compare the estimate with one of your own Earnings and Leave Statements, typed in or read once from its PDF and never kept.
Free and Premium

Schedule

  • Regular 5/8, AWS 4×10, 5/4/9 and rotating wheels.
  • Pay days, rest days, holidays, short days and leave on one calendar.
  • Pay period tracker, step-increase countdown with reminders, leave balances and a fitness tracker.
  • Export to Apple Calendar, and up to sixteen rotating wheels compared side by side.
Premium

Plan ahead

  • Career progression, every step and grade through your full performance level.
  • Pay raise calculator, TSP projector, FERS calculator, VERA / VSIP, leave cash-out and a retirement dashboard.
  • Compare two GS positions, or a GS salary with the private sector.

Free and Premium

The Calculator is free for everyone, with no account and no sign-in. Premium is a subscription bought through the App Store.

Free, always

  • The Calculator for every series, grade, step and locality.
  • The Regular 5×8 schedule with pay dates and holidays.
  • Step-increase notifications.

PayGrade Premium

  • All eleven Tools, the Career tab, and the Home countdowns and trackers.
  • The full Schedule: AWS and rotating-wheel schedules, leave and custom marks, and export to Apple Calendar.
  • A shareable pay-summary image.
  • Monthly or annual. The annual plan starts with a 3-day free trial. Current prices are on the App Store page and in the app.

If Premium lapses, nothing you entered is lost. The premium screens lock until you subscribe again.

Your data stays on your phone

PayGrade has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. Everything you enter stays on your device.

The one thing the app downloads is the year's pay tables and figures: at most once a day it fetches three small files we publish, each signed so the app can check that it came from us, and nothing about you goes the other way. The privacy policy says exactly what the app keeps, what it fetches, and what each optional permission is for.

PayGrade is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the U.S. Government, OPM, or any federal agency. Its figures are estimates; confirm anything that matters with your HR office or OPM.
