How we work

Three rules, and every product has to keep all of them.

From the source

Every figure is taken from the page its publisher puts it on, checked against that page, and updates itself when the publisher's figure changes. In PayGrade that means OPM's pay tables, the IRS's brackets, the TSP's limits and the SSA's figures.

Plain words

A screen says what a number is and where it came from. Our descriptions say what the software does today, not what we hope it will do next year.

Yours stays yours

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. Nothing you enter leaves your device, and nothing reaches us unless you send it yourself.

PayGrade: GS Pay Calculator

The pay calculator for U.S. federal employees on the General Schedule. Enter your series, grade, step and locality to see your base pay, locality adjustment and gross pay, estimate what you take home, keep your work schedule and plan your career, all from OPM's own tables, which the app keeps current by itself. For iPhone and iPad. The Calculator is free; Premium is a subscription through the App Store.
